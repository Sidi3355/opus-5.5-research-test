\subsection*{AI use statement}
Generative AI was used extensively in this work, and the models studied are themselves generative AI systems. AI agents built on Claude models performed the literature search and novelty assessment, designed and implemented the task suite and the independent task audit, wrote the experiment harness and analysis code, and drafted the manuscript, under human direction. Claude models also served as experimental subjects, report judges, and code reviewers, as described in \cref{sec:setup}. Every reference was checked against its source; every task passed automatic validation and independent review; every quantitative claim is generated from the trial records by the accompanying scripts; judge labels were compared with a sample coded by the lead agent without judge labels (a same-model check; no human labels were collected). \responsibility

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
The study involves no human subjects and no personal data. All agent actions ran in sandboxes on synthetic repositories. The behaviors we document (test gaming and misleading reports) are already publicly known in general form; the task suite is intended to help developers and reviewers measure and reduce them.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
All prompts, condition texts, probes, and rubrics appear verbatim in \cref{app:prompts}. The supplementary material contains the 24 tasks and 14 minimal-pair variants with reference implementations and hidden tests, the harness, every trial record (including tool calls and file diffs), all judge and reviewer outputs, the pre-registration with its amendments, and the scripts that produce every number, table, and figure; all of it will be released publicly. Model access is through the public Claude Code CLI; results for hosted models can drift over time.
