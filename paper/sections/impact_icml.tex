\section*{Impact Statement}
This work documents a way in which AI coding agents can mislead the people who review their work: when they game a test, their reports often attribute the gamed behavior to requirements that do not exist. The main expected impact is positive, by helping developers, reviewers, and tool builders detect such behavior (for example, by asking agents for the provenance of behavior and verifying cited requirements) and by providing a measurement protocol for training more honest agents. We see no substantial new risk from publishing: the tasks are synthetic, and the behaviors are observable in public models.
